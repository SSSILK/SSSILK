# 💫 About Me:
I’m $$$ILK, my software developer alias. I’m an IT Specialist with CompTIA A+, CompTIA Security+, and the Google IT Support Certificate, with an AAS in Computer Programming and extensive experience in software testing.

My path into technology started somewhere completely different. I began in food service, worked my way into supervision, and twice found myself being considered for the next step up. At 20, after deciding that path wasn’t for me, I left my job in another state and moved back home.

Music eventually became a major part of my life, and through it I discovered just how deeply I depended on computers. They were the machines behind the work, the experimentation, the production, and the ideas I was trying to bring to life. I became less interested in simply using them and more interested in understanding what was happening underneath—how systems worked, how to break down problems, how to optimize them, and how to rebuild them better.

That curiosity led me into IT, programming, and software development.

Now I’m building my own software with a particular philosophy: technology should be powerful without demanding that the user become a technician to take advantage of it. My current project puts advanced privacy, security, identity, and fingerprinting protections directly into the architecture, with those defenses operating automatically rather than requiring users to understand dozens of technical settings. It is designed to give users stronger control over their digital footprint while decreasing the learning curve and increasing the speed and usability expected from modern software/browsers.

I’m currently working on an important project that I hope to debut soon.

We all need money, so I put the $$$ in my name.
